Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Pill Scan ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Pill Scan mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Email address (for account creation and authentication)
  • Account user ID and authentication data from Google or Apple Sign-In (if used)
  • Profile name, if supplied by your sign-in provider
  • Feedback and support messages that you choose to submit

1.2 Medicine Data

  • Medicine names, dosage forms, and quantities
  • Expiration dates
  • Barcodes (when scanned)
  • Custom labels and notes
  • Medication schedules and adherence records

1.3 Technical Data

  • Device type and operating system
  • App and advertising interactions, diagnostic events, app version and operating system version
  • Crash reports, error messages and stack traces; our diagnostic records are linked to your account user ID and are not anonymous
  • Google AdMob may collect IP addresses (used to infer approximate location), advertising and other device identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostic information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Send expiration alerts and medication reminders
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Improve App performance and user experience
  • Respond to support requests

We do not:

  • Sell your personal data to third parties
  • Share your medicine data with advertisers
  • Use your health data for marketing purposes

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Cloud Storage

Your data is stored securely in Supabase cloud infrastructure:

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL)
  • Access to account-linked records is restricted using database access controls
  • Row-level security ensures you can only access your own data
  • Servers are located in secure data centers

3.2 Local Storage

The App stores preferences, reminder information and pending diagnostic events on your device. Local notifications are scheduled on the device. We do not claim that all local data is separately encrypted or automatically erased when you sign out. Exported PDF/CSV files and copies you share are under your control.

4. Data Sharing

We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With Supabase for database hosting, authentication and storage of account-linked diagnostic and feedback records
  • Advertising: Google AdMob processes technical and advertising data as described below
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • With Your Consent: If you explicitly agree to share data

4.1 Advertising

The App may display a banner supplied by Google AdMob on the Home screen. The App does not send medicine names, barcodes, labels, schedules, adherence records, notes, our Supabase account user IDs, or search terms to advertisers. Where required, the Google User Messaging Platform asks for your advertising privacy choices before an ad is requested. You can revisit available advertising privacy options from Settings. Ads are requested as non-personalized where supported by the release configuration.

Google AdMob processes the technical data listed in section 1.3 for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads can still involve device identifiers and other technical data. See Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosures.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all personal data we store about you
  • Export: Download your medicine data (PDF or CSV)
  • Correction: Update or correct your information
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact mezidosoft@gmail.com or use the available in-app controls. For account deletion instructions and requests, see Data deletion.

6. Data Retention

  • Your data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Completed account deletion removes your authentication account and associated application records from the active database, including medicines, schedules, adherence records, labels, feedback and diagnostics
  • Backup expiry and infrastructure log retention depend on the hosting configuration; we do not promise a fixed deletion period for all backups
  • Google processes and retains advertising data under its own policy; deleting a Pill Scan account does not itself delete Google-held data
  • Copies you export or share are not deleted by account deletion

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication (see Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In: Authentication (see Google Privacy Policy)
  • Apple Sign-In: Authentication (see Apple Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob and UMP: Advertising and advertising privacy choices (see Google Privacy Policy)

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or via email. The updated date identifies the latest revision. Where consent is required, a policy update does not replace that consent.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact us at:

Email: mezidosoft@gmail.com

GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have additional rights:

  • Legal Basis: We process your data based on your consent and contractual necessity
  • Data Controller: Pill Scan is the data controller for your personal information
  • Right to Object: You can object to processing at any time
  • Right to Complain: You may file a complaint with your local data protection authority
